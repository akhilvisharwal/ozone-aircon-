Essentials

Understanding treated fresh air systems

Treated Fresh Air systems continuously supply filtered outdoor air into your building while removing stale, contaminated air. This mechanical ventilation approach ensures consistent oxygen levels and removes pollutants that accumulate in sealed modern spaces.

TFA systems engineered for balanced indoor air

Treated Fresh Air (TFA) systems are sophisticated mechanical systems designed to introduce fresh outdoor air into indoor spaces while simultaneously maintaining optimal comfort levels and superior air quality. These advanced systems work by filtering, conditioning, and carefully regulating incoming air to effectively remove harmful pollutants, control humidity levels, and ensure a consistent and reliable supply of clean, breathable air throughout the building. By integrating multiple components such as intake vents, filtration units, temperature controls, and humidity management systems, TFA systems create a healthier indoor environment that supports occupant well-being and productivity while reducing the concentration of contaminants and allergens that can accumulate in sealed indoor spaces.

Balanced ventilation

Fresh outdoor air filtered and conditioned to match your indoor comfort needs.

Seamless integration

Definition

What treated fresh air does for your space

Treated Fresh Air brings outdoor air into your building while filtering out pollutants, allergens, and contaminants. It maintains proper oxygen levels and humidity balance for occupants who spend most of their time indoors.

Pollutant removal

Removes dust, allergens, and harmful airborne particles before air enters your occupied spaces.

Humidity balance

Maintains optimal humidity levels to prevent discomfort, condensation, and structural issues.

Science

Why treated fresh air matters now

Modern buildings trap stale air. TFA systems pull outdoor air in, filter it clean, and circulate it through your space while your HVAC maintains comfort.

Energy recovery

Heat exchangers reclaim thermal energy from exhaust air to pre-condition fresh air intake for efficiency.

Airtight buildings

Modern airtight construction makes mechanical fresh air supply essential for healthy occupancy.
