Indoor climate

Indoor units engineered for every space

From ceiling cassettes to floor-standing models, each unit connects to a single outdoor system and adapts to your specific room layout and design preferences. Choose the configuration that fits your space, your comfort needs, and your vision for how your environment should look and feel.

Systems

High Static Ductable

High Static Ductable Units are advanced indoor air conditioning systems designed for large spaces that require powerful airflow and long duct runs. These units are ideal for projects where air needs to be distributed evenly across multiple rooms or large areas through an extensive duct network.

Compact

Ceiling cassette units for balanced comfort

These versatile units mount flush directly into standard drop ceilings, delivering exceptionally quiet and even air distribution across entire rooms without any protruding elements or visible ductwork. They are ideal for offices, retail spaces, and residential areas where both aesthetic appeal and reliable performance matter equally, ensuring that spaces maintain their visual design integrity while providing superior comfort. The seamless integration into existing ceiling systems makes them a popular choice for renovations and new construction projects alike, offering contractors and architects a flexible solution that adapts to various architectural styles and spatial requirements. Their discreet installation method also allows for easy access to ceiling plenums when maintenance or future modifications are needed.

Concealed

Ceiling concealed units for hidden efficiency

Ducted systems represent a comprehensive climate control solution that works quietly behind the scenes, distributing precisely conditioned air throughout your building's infrastructure via an extensive network of concealed ducts. These sophisticated systems are particularly well-suited for new construction projects or major renovation undertakings where seamless integration into the overall building design is the primary goal. By utilizing existing wall cavities, floor spaces, and ceiling plenums, ducted systems deliver consistent temperature control to multiple rooms and zones while maintaining a clean, uncluttered aesthetic throughout your living or working spaces.

Wall Mounted

Split air conditioner units for flexible placement

A split AC unit is a standalone air conditioning system that requires no ceiling modifications and offers easy installation and maintenance. It works well in spaces where traditional ductwork isn't practical, providing precise temperature control in individual rooms or specific areas. Its self-contained design makes it ideal for retrofitting existing spaces or customizing cooling and heating solutions for homes and businesses.
