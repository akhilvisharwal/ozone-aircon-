Founded

Built on experience, driven by precision

Ozone Aircon has spent decades mastering the craft of climate control across Delhi NCR. What began as a vision for reliable HVAC solutions has grown into a trusted partnership with hundreds of residential and commercial clients who depend on our systems to keep their spaces comfortable year-round. We don't just install air conditioning—we engineer environments that work.

Leadership

Our founder

Mr. Kanwarpal Singh established Ozone Aircon with a focus on thoughtful engineering, practical design, and dependable execution. With decades of experience in the HVAC industry, his work has been shaped by a deep understanding of climate systems, technical precision, and the importance of long-term performance.

His approach emphasizes quality, functionality, and careful attention to detail—principles that continue to influence the company’s work today. With a steady commitment to engineering comfort through well-designed systems, his vision remains rooted in creating spaces that perform efficiently, quietly, and reliably over time.

People

The people who make it work

At Ozone Aircon, our strength is defined by the expertise and discipline of our people. Our team of technicians, engineers, and project specialists approach every project with a commitment to precision, performance, and uncompromising quality.

Our technicians bring years of hands-on experience, executing installations with accuracy and attention to detail. Their understanding of airflow, system dynamics, and efficiency ensures that every installation is not only functional, but built for long-term reliability.

Our engineers take a thoughtful and analytical approach, carefully designing systems that are tailored to each space. From precise load calculations to optimized configurations, every decision is guided by performance, efficiency, and durability.

Our project specialists ensure seamless coordination at every stage. With a focus on planning, timing, and communication, they maintain a smooth and structured workflow, ensuring that each project is delivered to the highest standard.

Together, we operate as a unified team—focused on delivering HVAC solutions that reflect reliability, technical excellence, and lasting performance. This commitment is at the core of everything we do.
