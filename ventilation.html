Ventilation design and installation for Delhi NCR

Modern ventilation systems that deliver fresh, filtered air throughout your space

Systems

How ventilation transforms your indoor environment

Proper ventilation removes stale air, controls humidity, and filters out contaminants. This creates healthier spaces where people breathe easier and feel more comfortable throughout the day.

Air handling units

AHUs are the heart of any ventilation system, moving and conditioning air efficiently. They work continuously to maintain optimal temperature and humidity levels in your space.

Advanced filtration

MERV-rated filters capture dust, pollen, and allergens before air circulates through your building. Regular filter maintenance ensures peak performance and cleaner air year-round.

Ductwork design

Properly designed ducts distribute air evenly without noise or energy loss. Our engineers calculate every bend and junction to maximize comfort and efficiency.

Components

The essentials of proper ventilation

Every system needs the right parts working together seamlessly

AHU

Air handling unit

The central unit that moves, filters, heats, and cools air before distributing it throughout your building via the duct network.

Filtration

MERV filtration system

High-efficiency filters remove particulates, allergens, and contaminants before conditioned air reaches occupied spaces.

Ductwork

Distribution network

Engineered duct layouts carry conditioned air from the AHU to every room, designed for minimal pressure loss and quiet operation.

Controls

Smart ventilation controls

Sensors and building management systems monitor air quality and occupancy, adjusting ventilation rates automatically for optimal efficiency.

Types

Ventilation approaches for every building

Different spaces demand different solutions. We match the right system to your needs.

Natural ventilation uses windows and openings

Fresh air flows through your space without mechanical assistance.

Mechanical ventilation provides controlled fan-driven airflow

Fans ensure consistent air movement regardless of weather.

Mixed-mode ventilation combines natural and mechanical systems

You get flexibility to switch between methods as conditions change.
